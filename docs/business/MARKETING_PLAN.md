# LastMinute — strategic marketing plan (first 6 months, launch city Pune)

## 1. Positioning

**For people who need something done today**, LastMinute finds a trusted, home-based expert nearby who can finish it in time — and books the cheapest reliable pickup for you.
**For skilled people working from home**, LastMinute brings paying customers from your neighbourhood, in a simple app that speaks your language.

Tagline: **"Need it now? Get it done."** · Hindi: **"अभी चाहिए? हो जाएगा।"**

Brand voice: warm, quick, a little cheeky, always respectful of the "aunties and uncles" who make the magic happen. Visual identity: yellow + ink, bold cards, real hands at work.

## 3. Channel plan

### Customers (demand)
| Channel | What we do | Budget share |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram Reels** | 3/week: "saved by LastMinute" mini-stories (blouse in 3 hrs, midnight cake), before/after, "guess the price"; Reel ads targeted within 8 km of launch areas; collab Reels with local creators (ASCI disclosure "#ad") | 30% |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Re-cut Reels + 60-sec "meet the maker" profiles | 5% |
| **Google Search & Maps** | Ads on "urgent tailor near me", "same day blouse stitching", "midnight cake delivery Pune"; Google Business Profile with reviews | 20% |
| **WhatsApp** | Opt-in channel with weekly "today's quick-ready makers"; referral link sharing (₹50 credit both sides) | 5% |
| **Housing societies & colleges** | RWA notice boards and WhatsApp groups (with admin permission), college fest stalls, wedding-season kiosks at saree/fabric markets | 15% |
| **PR & community** | Local news stories on older women earning from home; Pune startup events | 5% |

### Providers (supply)
| Channel | What we do | Budget share |
|---|---|---|
| **Field onboarding** | Agents visit markets, tailoring material shops, sewing-machine dealers and SHG meetings; set up the shop for the provider in 10 minutes | 10% |
| **Partnerships** | Women's self-help groups (DAY-NRLM), NGOs for women & senior livelihoods, cooking & tailoring classes, sewing-machine brands | 5% |
| **WhatsApp + YouTube in Hindi/Marathi** | 60-sec "how to get your first order" videos, read-aloud style; partner-recruitment video | 5% |
| **"Bring a friend"** | ₹100 bonus when a referred provider finishes 3 orders | in field budget |

**Suggested first-6-month budget:** ₹18 L total (≈ ₹3 L/month), split as above. Keep 10% of every channel for experiments.

## 4. Content pillars

1. **Saved in time** — real customer stories with a countdown clock on screen.
2. **Meet the maker** — the people behind the work; dignity and pride, never pity.
3. **How it works** — 15-second explainers: book → rider picks up → done.
4. **Money tips for makers** — pricing, rush fees, photos of work, reviews.
5. **Trust & safety** — verified makers, privacy, pay after work.

## 6. 90-day launch plan

| Weeks | Focus | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Onboard providers in 3 neighbourhoods; record 20 maker stories; set up Google Business, Instagram, WhatsApp channel | 300 verified providers |
| 5–8 | Soft launch to 5 housing societies + 2 colleges; referral program; first Reel ads | 2,000 orders/month run-rate |
| 9–12 | Creator collabs, wedding-season push, PR story | 5,000 orders/month run-rate, 4.6★ average |

## 7. KPIs

- **Demand:** installs, sign-up rate, first-order rate, orders/customer/month (target 1.8), CAC (≤ ₹120), referral share (≥ 25% of new users).
- **Supply:** active providers, acceptance within 10 min (≥ 80%), on-time completion (≥ 95%), provider earnings/month, provider churn.
- **Marketplace:** fill rate, average deadline met, rating, repeat rate at 30 days (≥ 40%).
- **Brand:** Reel completion rate, saves/shares, branded search volume.

## 8. Rules we follow

- **Marketing consent:** messages only to people who opted in (DPDP Act); one-tap unsubscribe.
- **SMS:** DLT-registered headers and templates (TRAI TCCCPR).
- **WhatsApp:** opt-in only, Business API templates.
- **Influencers:** clear "#ad / paid partnership" disclosures (ASCI guidelines).
- **Claims:** no fake reviews or invented numbers; testimonials with written consent of the person shown.
