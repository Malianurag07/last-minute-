# LastMinute — business model & unit economics

> All numbers below are **planning assumptions for a first city (Pune)**, to be validated in a 90-day pilot. They are not results.

## The problem

- People often need small jobs done **today**: a blouse before a wedding, a cake for a surprise, a zip before a flight, a saree draped in an hour.
- The people best placed to help — **tailors, home bakers, cooks, mehendi artists and repair people working from home** — are nearly invisible online. Many are older or new to smartphones, so marketplaces built for tech-savvy professionals leave them out.
- Moving the item is the other blocker: customers don't know which delivery app is cheapest and most reliable right now.

## The solution

1. Customers find a **nearby home-based expert who can finish before their deadline**.
2. The app **compares Rapido, Porter, Uber and Ola** on price, reliability and time, and opens the best one with the addresses ready.
3. Providers get a **simple, large-text, read-aloud app** (Hindi + English) to accept work, see earnings, and team up with other providers.

## How we earn

| Stream | Rate | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| Commission on the service price | **12%** (only **5% in a provider's first 3 months**) | Provider (deducted from payout) |
| Share of the rush fee (orders due within 3 hours) | **20%** of the rush fee; provider keeps 80% | Provider |
| Convenience fee | **₹9 per order** | Customer |
| *Later:* Provider Pro | ₹149/month — featured listing, WhatsApp catalogue, extra analytics | Provider (optional) |
| *Later:* Delivery margin | 5–8% once direct B2B APIs with delivery partners are signed | Delivery partner |
| *Later:* Bulk/B2B orders | Boutiques, event planners, caterers | Business customers |

Delivery is a **pass-through** today: the customer pays the delivery partner directly in their app, so we carry no delivery cost or risk.

## Unit economics per order (steady state)

| Line | Assumption | ₹ per order |
|---|---|---|
| Average service price | ₹380 | — |
| Commission | 12% × ₹380 | 45.6 |
| Rush share | 35% of orders are rush × ₹60 avg rush fee × 20% | 4.2 |
| Convenience fee | flat | 9.0 |
| **Net revenue** | ≈ 15.5% of service value | **58.8** |
| SMS / notifications | | −0.5 |
| Cloud & tools | | −1.0 |
| Customer support | 1 ticket per 15 orders × ₹90 | −6.0 |
| Promotions & referral credits | year-1 average | −12.0 |
| **Contribution** | ≈ 67% of net revenue | **≈ 39** |

**Provider view:** on a ₹450 blouse with a ₹50 rush fee, the provider receives **₹450 + ₹40 − ₹54 = ₹436** — and a customer they would never have found otherwise.

## Growth targets (one city)

| | Month 3 | Month 6 | Month 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active providers | 300 | 800 | 2,000 |
| Orders / month | 2,000 | 8,000 | 25,000 |
| GMV / month (service value) | ₹7.6 L | ₹30 L | ₹95 L |
| Net revenue / month | ₹1.2 L | ₹4.7 L | ₹14.7 L |

- **Break-even:** fixed costs ≈ ₹10.5 L/month (team of 8, field ops, tech) ÷ ₹39 contribution ≈ **27,000 orders/month** (≈ 900/day) — reachable around month 12–14 in one city.
- **Customer acquisition cost:** ≈ ₹120 blended (referrals + Reels). At 1.8 orders/month × ₹39, payback is **under 2 months**.
- **Provider acquisition:** ≈ ₹250 each (field agents in markets and housing societies, WhatsApp groups, self-help groups).

## Why it can win

- **Supply nobody else has:** home-based, often older providers, onboarded in their language with an app built for them.
- **Urgency = willingness to pay:** rush fees make the "last-minute" moment profitable for both sides.
- **Asset-light:** no warehouses, no fleet; delivery rides on existing networks.
- **Network effects within a neighbourhood:** more providers → faster fulfilment → more customers → more providers. Collaborations (tailor + mehendi + beauty for a wedding) create bundles competitors can't copy easily.
- **Trust & compliance as a moat:** DPDPA-ready privacy, verified providers, audited admin actions, WCAG-accessible design.

## Competition

| | Focus | Gap we fill |
|---|---|---|
| Urban Company | Trained professionals at your home, scheduled services | Doesn't serve home-based micro-businesses or same-hour deadlines |
| Swiggy / Zomato | Restaurant food | Not services; no home cooks for custom orders at scale |
| Justdial / Google Maps | Listings | No booking, no deadline promise, no delivery comparison |
| WhatsApp / word of mouth | Informal | No discovery beyond your circle, no trust signals |

## Ask (illustrative)

**₹1.5 Cr pre-seed for 18 months:** 45% team & product, 30% provider onboarding and customer growth, 10% trust, safety & compliance (pentest, ISO 27001, legal), 15% buffer. Milestone: one city at break-even and a second city launched.
