Record in the founder's voice, calm and confident. Timecodes match `LastMinute_Investor_Pitch.mp4`; speak the line when its scene appears. Add the audio in any editor (CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci) on top of the music, music at about −18 dB under the voice.

| Time | Scene | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Title | Hi, I'm [name], founder of LastMinute. Need it now? We get it done. |

India's gig and platform workforce is set to triple this decade, per NITI Aayog — but these home experts are being left out. |
| 0:34 | Solution | LastMinute is one app for "I need it now". You find a home-based expert near you who can finish before your deadline.
